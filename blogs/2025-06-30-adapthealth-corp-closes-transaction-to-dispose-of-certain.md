CONSHOHOCKEN, Pa. – June 30, 2025 – AdaptHealth Corp. (NASDAQ: AHCO) (“AdaptHealth” or the “Company”), a national leader in providing patient-centered, healthcare-at-home solutions including home medical equipment, medical supplies, and related services, today announced that it has closed on the previously disclosed disposition of certain infusion assets in its Wellness at Home segment to a third party.
