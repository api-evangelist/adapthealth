PLYMOUTH MEETING, Pa. – May 11, 2023 ‐ AdaptHealth Corp. (NASDAQ: AHCO) a national leader in providing patient‐centered, healthcare‐at‐home solutions including home medical equipment, medical supplies, and related services, has entered into an agreement to become the capitated provider of durable home medical equipment and supplies to Humana Medicare Advantage HMO members in 33 states, plus the District of Columbia.
