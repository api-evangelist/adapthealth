PLYMOUTH MEETING, Pa. – July 2, 2024 - AdaptHealth Corp. (NASDAQ: AHCO) (“AdaptHealth” or the “Company”) , a national leader in providing patient-centered, healthcare-at-home solutions including home medical equipment, medical supplies, and related services, announced today the appointment of Dale Wolf as Chair of the Board of Directors, effective July 1, 2024.
