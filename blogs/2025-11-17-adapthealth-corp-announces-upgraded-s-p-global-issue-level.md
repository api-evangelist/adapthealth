CONSHOHOCKEN, Pa. – November 17, 2025 - AdaptHealth Corp. (NASDAQ: AHCO) (“AdaptHealth” or the “Company”), a national leader in providing patient-centered, healthcare-at-home solutions including home medical equipment, medical supplies, and related services, announced today that S&P Global Ratings has recently upgraded its issue-level rating on the Company’s senior unsecured debt to ‘BB-’ from ‘B+’ and revised its recovery rating to ‘4’ from ‘5’.
