PLYMOUTH MEETING, Pa. – December 3, 2024 - AdaptHealth Corp. (NASDAQ: AHCO) (“AdaptHealth” or the “Company”), a national leader in providing patient-centered, healthcare-at-home solutions including home medical equipment, medical supplies, and related services, announced today that Russell Schuster has been appointed Chief Commercial Officer, effective December 2nd, 2024.
